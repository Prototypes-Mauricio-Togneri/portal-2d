Chamber detail mixer

Fourteen layers that stop a chamber looking like one tile stamped everywhere. Switch them on in any combination. The chamber is drawn by the game’s own renderer from your sprites, so this is what the game would show.

My pick

Tone first, wear second. On the floor: tile shade, large patches and room light, with wear marks on one tile in ten. On the walls: large patches and a light panel shade.

Left out of it: grime, which muddies the clean lab look, and wear on the walls, which barely shows at this size.

  • Tone carries at any size

    At 45 px a tile, shade and light still read from across the chamber. Fine marks mostly vanish, so they cannot do the job alone.

  • Sparse reads as wear

    A mark on one tile in ten looks like history. A mark on every other tile looks like noise, and the floor stops being a calm ground for the puzzle.

  • Nothing that looks like a rule

    Every layer is grey and low in contrast, and wall marks keep off the sloped edges where portals open, so nothing passes for a rivet, an arrow or an antline.
