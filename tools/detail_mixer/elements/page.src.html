Portal 2D · element look

Element detail mixer

Wear and tone for the ten element sprites, so no two boxes, buttons, switches, arrows or ditches look alike. Every layer is drawn in code over your existing sprites by the game’s own renderer, only where a sprite already has pixels, so shapes and shadows stay as they are.

My pick

Tone and room light on every instance, then fine wear on most of them, kept to the plain metal and paint. The page opens on it, on the playground, the one chamber with all seven things.

Left out of it: dust, the domes’ finish and the mirror’s glass. Try “Faint, everywhere” for your floor’s own recipe applied to things.

  • Tone tells them apart

    At 45 px a scratch is one pixel. A box a shade warmer than its neighbour still reads from across the chamber, so tone does most of the work.

  • Wear on the plain parts only

    Plates, clamps, blocks, paint and pit sides take the wear. Red, yellow and the mirror are put back as drawn after it; the meaning checks below measure what is left.

  • Most, not all

    Each kind of wear lands on half to four in five instances, so the marks vary from one to the next instead of repeating.

    Once it looks right, copy these settings and paste them back to Claude, or send screenshots of the rail: every value there is printed as a number.
